Way Out

A game by Quách Thiếu Khang

You wake up in an abandoned hospital. The emergency exit is locked by 5 keys scattered across its wards — never in the same place twice.
And you are not alone. The eye at the top shows how close it is to noticing you.

  • WASD Move
  • Shift Sprint — drains stamina, very loud
  • Z Walk quietly (hold) — it can hear normal footsteps from 5 m
  • C Crouch (hold) — sneak, and crawl through vents it cannot follow you into
  • F Flashlight — when it is on, it can see you from farther away
  • Q Flash burst — stuns it if it is in your beam (costs battery)
  • Space (mash) Break free when it grabs you
  • R Sense the nearest key — an arrow shows the way for a few seconds (then the exit, once it is open)
  • E Pick up / hide in a locker — it will not find you unless it saw you get in
  • G / Click Throw a bottle or can you picked up — the noise lures it away. Bottles are loud and smash; cans are quieter but you can pick them up again
  • Crouch behind a bed and it cannot see you; shelves block its view
  • Space Jump
  • Esc Pause

Settings

Headphones recommended — its footsteps tell you which way it is coming from.
