Way Out needs a keyboard and a mouse.

Headphones recommended — its footsteps tell you which way it is coming from.

Hollow Pines Hospital closed in 1987, after the night shift on Ward C never clocked out. The papers called it a fire. There was no fire.

Thirty-nine years later you came looking for a story. You woke on a gurney with your flashlight still in your hand. The chain on the door behind you is new.

Pages of a doctor's journal lie scattered across the wards, and old tapes still sit where they were dropped. Read enough of them and you will learn what is walking the wards, and how this ends.

Find the five keys. Reach the exit. Do not let it smile at you.
